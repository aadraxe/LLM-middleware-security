Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks. A Security Operations Center, or SOC, is a centralized team responsible for monitoring and responding to security incidents. SOC analysts use tools like SIEM platforms to correlate logs and detect threats.
The incident response lifecycle typically includes preparation, detection and analysis, containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident activity. The MITRE ATT&CK framework is a knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques based on real-world observations. It is widely used by defenders to map and understand attacker behavior.
Common attack types include phishing, where attackers trick users into revealing credentials, and malware, which is malicious software designed to damage or gain unauthorized access. Multi-factor authentication, or MFA, adds an extra layer of security beyond passwords. Threat hunting is the proactive search for threats that have evaded existing security controls.
